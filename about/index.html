About Me :¨ ·.· ¨:
                      `· . ꔫ

I am a dedicated and passionate student from Alor Setar, Kedah, currently in my fourth semester of a degree in Information Content Management at UiTM Puncak Perdana. Prior to this, I completed my diploma in Information Management at UiTM Segamat, Johor. With one more semester to go before my internship, I am eager to continue expanding my knowledge and plan to pursue a master’s degree in the future. My inspiration for further studies comes from my genuine enjoyment of this field and my curiosity to explore more about information and content management.

Background & Interests ♛

My career goal is to work in the IT industry, preferably at Petronas or in a company based in Putrajaya, whether in the government or private sector. I am highly interested in information management, digital content strategy, and how technology can be used to streamline processes and improve efficiency.

Beyond academics, I am an active person who enjoys playing and watching sports. I also love participating in charity events and spending quality time with family and friends. These experiences shape my values of teamwork, community engagement, and continuous self-improvement. I look forward to applying my skills and passion in a professional setting where I can contribute meaningfully to the industry.

Family Background🧸

Family means everything to me, and I’m grateful to be part of a close-knit, loving, and supportive household. I come from a wonderful family of five my parents, an older brother, and a younger sister. Though we have faced ups and downs, our bond remains unbreakable.

My mother works as an Account Analyst at KWSP, while my father, now retired, was an English Lecturer at UNISHAM. Their hard work and dedication have always been an inspiration to me.

My older brother is an Engineer at Aerodyne Group, and his wife, my sister-in-law, is an Accountant at Standard Chartered. They have blessed our family with two adorable boys who bring so much joy into our lives. My younger sister is currently pursuing her studies in Aerospace Engineering at UIA, following her dreams in the aerospace industry.

Although I lost my younger brother, his memory remains with us, and he will always be a part of our hearts.

As a family, we cherish every moment together whether it’s traveling, celebrating festivals, or simply enjoying family gatherings. The thing I love most about my family is how we always support and stand by each other, no matter what. Our home is always filled with laughter, love, and meaningful moments that I treasure deeply.
